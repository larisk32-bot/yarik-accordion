MUSICAL REWARDS — YARIK AND THE MAGIC ACCORDION

Nine 10-second educational melody excerpts, rendered by this game's own Web Audio accordion synthesizer. Only the right-hand melodic voice is used; highest notes of simultaneous chords are retained. These are adaptations, not full scores or commercial recordings. Rhythmic proportions are retained; excerpt tempos are adjusted to fit ten seconds. Different gates present different portions of each work.

FINALE: "A Melody for Yarik" / "Мелодия для Ярика" — an original waltz composed for this game by Mira, 2026.
24 bars, 3/4, quarter note = 72, exactly 60 seconds. Original melody and accordion-style bass/chord accompaniment are encoded in music.js. Familiar tonal waltz harmony is used; no specific existing melody was requested or copied.

No third-party sound recordings are included. The source note data and attribution above concern only the music, not the rest of the game code or illustrations.
